Interior Designer

Furniture

Drag furniture onto the 2D plan.

Draw wall: drag from start to end.
Hold Shift for horizontal/vertical walls.

Add door: click a wall.
Select / Move: drag furniture or a door.
R: rotate furniture or flip door swing.
Delete: remove selection.

Mouse wheel: zoom.
Space + drag or right-drag: pan.

The 2.5D preview uses 1.1 m cutaway walls so you can see inside.

Draw a room or modify the example layout. All measurements are in metres.

Editor help

Edit mode: furniture, walls, doors and windows. Room mode: draw rooms; click a room to zoom in, drag it to move.
Shortcuts: Q switches Edit/Room mode. Edit mode: 1 Select, 2 Wall, 3 Door, 4 Window, 5 Stairs. Room mode: 1 Select, 2 Room. F fits the view. Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicate, Ctrl/Cmd+C copy, Ctrl/Cmd+V paste furniture, Ctrl/Cmd+X delete the selection.
Draw wall: drag from start to end.
Draw room: drag between opposite corners; corners snap to grid and to nearby wall faces.
Grid precision: hold Alt/Option for rougher snapping (×10), or Ctrl/Cmd for finer snapping (÷10).
• Wall ends automatically snap to nearby walls & corners.
• Connection blocks can be moved, selected, and deleted; crossing walls are blocked.
• Hold Shift for orthogonal lock.

Select / Move: click furniture then drag.
• Dragged furniture snaps flush against nearby walls; hold Ctrl/Cmd while dragging to place it freely.
• Hold Shift and click to multi-select; drag empty space to marquee select.
• Arrow keys nudge the selected furniture by 1 screen pixel (hold Shift for 10 pixels); zoom in for finer steps.
• Clicking a grouped item selects its whole group; pick a member in the group list to edit it alone.
• Right-click furniture to rename, recolour, duplicate, copy, paste, group, ungroup, allow overlap, use a custom model (OBJ or image) or delete.
• Items marked Allow overlap (e.g. tables) sit underneath; furniture placed on them is raised onto them in 3D.
• Create a named group from selected furniture in the right panel.
• Blue dot: rotate furniture or a selected wall (snaps to 45°; hold Ctrl/Cmd for free rotation).
• Square handles: resize furniture or rooms from any edge or corner; hold Shift to keep the aspect ratio, Ctrl/Cmd to scale symmetrically around the centre.
• Furniture inside a door's swing area counts as overlapping and turns red.
• Layers: for items marked Allow overlap, use the Layer row in the right-click menu; higher layers sit on top of lower ones (tables, desks and chairs allow overlap by default).
• Drag orange wall-end handles to stretch; select a joint and press × to detach.
• Red highlight: furniture overlaps walls or other furniture.
Add door: click a wall. Doors can sit side by side to form a double door.
Add stairs: click to place; they appear on the floor they lead to. Press R to switch up/down.
Materials: pick a floor material for a room and a material for a wall in the Selection panel; costs appear in the price estimator.
R: rotate furniture or walls by 45°; cycle door hinge side and swing direction; switch stairs up/down.
Delete: remove selection.
Trackpad: pinch to zoom; two-finger scroll to pan.
Mouse wheel: zoom.
Space + drag: pan (2D).
3D preview: left‑drag rotates around room, right‑drag pans, wheel zooms; click a room to zoom into it.
